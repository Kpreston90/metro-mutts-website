# Research Notes — Does Your Dog Actually Need Friends?

- Merck Veterinary Manual describes dogs as having flexible social structures and notes that individual social relationships vary. It also explains that dogs communicate with visual signals, vocalizations, scent, and touch.
- Best Friends Animal Society emphasizes reading the whole dog rather than treating one cue as definitive. Loose posture can indicate comfort, while a frozen body, tucked tail, moving away, or certain displacement behaviors can signal discomfort or stress.
- The article will avoid medical diagnosis, avoid promising daycare is right for every dog, and frame social preference as individual.
